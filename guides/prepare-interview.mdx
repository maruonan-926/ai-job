Walking into an interview prepared is the single biggest factor within your control. The Autumn Job Workbench turns any job description into a personalized preparation plan — generating likely questions, suggesting reference answers grounded in the role's requirements, and running mock sessions that simulate the real conversation. By the time you sit down with a hiring team, you'll have already practiced the questions they're most likely to ask.

## Prepare for Your Next Interview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the JD match result for your target role">
    Navigate to the application record for the role you're preparing for and open its **JD Match** tab. If you haven't run a match yet, paste the job description and click **Analyze** now. The match result is the foundation the interview prep engine builds on — it identifies the skills, responsibilities, and competencies the employer cares about most.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate interview questions from the JD">
    Click **Generate Interview Questions**. The AI reads the job description and produces a structured question set organized by category: **Behavioral**, **Technical / Functional**, **Situational**, and **Culture & Motivation**. The number and mix of questions reflects the seniority and function of the role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review AI-suggested reference answers">
    Expand any question to see its reference answer — a model response that incorporates the keywords and competencies from the JD. Reference answers are frameworks, not scripts. Read them to understand what a strong answer addresses, then plan your own response using examples from your actual experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a mock interview session">
    Click **Start Mock Session** to begin a timed practice run. Choose **Text Mode** to type your answers or **Audio Mode** to speak them aloud (audio is transcribed automatically). The session presents questions one at a time under realistic conditions — you can't skip back to edit a previous answer mid-session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review AI feedback on each answer">
    After the session, the AI scores each answer across four dimensions: **Relevance** (did you address what was asked?), **Structure** (STAR or equivalent framework), **Specificity** (concrete examples vs. vague generalities), and **Keyword Alignment** (did you use language that mirrors the JD?). Each score comes with a brief explanation and a suggested improvement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log the real interview result and request an AI debrief">
    After the actual interview, return to the application record, set the status to reflect the outcome, and add your notes while details are fresh. Click **Request AI Debrief** to compare what was actually asked against your prep questions, and to get a reflection summary you can use to improve your approach for the next round or the next role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Save the generated question set by clicking **Save to Prep Library**. The night before your interview, open the library and review your planned answers one more time without running a full mock session. A quick review under low pressure consolidates what you've practiced and helps you sleep better.
</Tip>

## Make the Most of Mock Sessions

Run your first mock session early — at least two or three days before the real interview — so you have time to act on the feedback. Repeat the session with the same question set after revising your answers; the AI tracks score changes across sessions so you can see concrete improvement. For senior or competitive roles, run at least one audio-mode session to practice speaking your answers aloud at a natural pace.

<Note>
  Mock sessions are private and never shared outside your account. Audio recordings are transcribed and then deleted within 24 hours — only the text transcript and feedback are stored.
</Note>
