The application tracker is your central command center for every role you pursue. Rather than scattering notes across spreadsheets, email threads, and sticky notes, the workbench consolidates the full hiring lifecycle — status, contacts, timelines, and outcomes — into a single, filterable board. Keeping it current means you'll never miss a follow-up, forget an interview date, or lose track of where you stand with a recruiter.

## Track Every Application from Start to Finish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a new application record">
    Click **+ New Application** from the board view or the list view. Enter the company name and role title. The workbench creates a card and assigns it a unique record ID, which you can reference in any export or integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the initial status">
    Choose the status that reflects where you are right now: **Wishlist**, **Applied**, **Screening**, **Interviewing**, **Offer**, or **Closed**. Setting an accurate starting status ensures your pipeline metrics stay reliable from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add details — channel, base city, and notes">
    Fill in the **Application Channel** (job board, referral, direct outreach, recruiter), the **Base City** for the role, and any initial notes. Record the date you applied so you can track response time and decide when to follow up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as priority">
    Toggle the **Priority** flag on any role that deserves extra attention — a dream company, a time-sensitive deadline, or a role with an upcoming interview. Priority applications surface at the top of filtered views so they're always visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move to the next stage as you progress">
    Each time you receive an update — a recruiter screen, a technical round, a hiring manager call — drag the card to its new status column or update the status field directly. Add a timestamped note explaining what happened and what the next step is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log the outcome">
    When a process concludes, set the status to **Offer** or **Closed**. For offers, link through to the offer evaluator. For closed roles, record the reason if you know it — this data helps you spot patterns across your search over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep Notes Fresh After Every Interaction

The most useful application records are the ones updated immediately after a call or interview, before details fade. After each recruiter or hiring manager touchpoint, open the record and add a note with:

- **Who you spoke with** and their title
- **Key topics covered** and any concerns they raised
- **What you committed to** (e.g., sending a portfolio link, completing a take-home)
- **The agreed next step** and its expected timeline

<Tip>
  Use the **Reminder** field to schedule a follow-up nudge. If a recruiter says "we'll be in touch within a week," set a reminder for seven days out so you know exactly when it's appropriate to reach back out.
</Tip>

## Filter the Board to Focus on What Matters

When your tracker grows to dozens of applications, filtering keeps it manageable. Use the board's filter bar to narrow your view by:

- **Status** — show only active applications (Screening, Interviewing) to focus your energy
- **Priority** — surface your top-tier roles instantly
- **Base City** — compare opportunities by location when evaluating relocation trade-offs
- **Date Applied** — spot stale applications that may need a follow-up or a close

<Note>
  Archiving closed or withdrawn applications removes them from your active board without deleting any data. You can restore or review archived records at any time from the **Archive** tab.
</Note>
