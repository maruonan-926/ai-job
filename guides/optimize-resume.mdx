A single static resume rarely performs well across every role you apply to. Applicant tracking systems (ATS) filter candidates based on keyword density and structural signals long before a human reviewer sees your application. The Autumn Job Workbench resume optimizer combines an AI-powered baseline score with job-description-specific matching so you can tailor your resume intelligently — not just stuff it with keywords — and arrive at each application with the strongest possible document.

## Optimize Your Resume Step by Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your resume">
    Navigate to **Resume Management** and click **Upload Resume**. The workbench accepts PDF and DOCX formats. Once uploaded, your resume is parsed into sections (Summary, Experience, Education, Skills) so the AI can analyze each part individually. You can store multiple versions and label them for easy reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run an AI score to identify weaknesses">
    Select a resume and click **Run AI Score**. The scorer evaluates your document across several dimensions: impact language, quantification of achievements, formatting clarity, section completeness, and keyword density. Each dimension receives a score with a plain-language explanation of what's holding it back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the target job description to run a match">
    Open the **JD Match** tab, paste the full job description for the role you're targeting, and click **Analyze**. The workbench compares your resume against the JD to produce a fit percentage, a list of matched skills and keywords, and a list of missing or underrepresented terms that the employer is explicitly looking for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the AI optimizer to propose rewrites">
    Click **Optimize for This JD**. The AI generates suggested rewrites for your experience bullets using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) where your originals are vague, and surfaces missing keywords in context — showing you exactly which bullet or section each term belongs in. Suggestions are presented inline, side by side with your original text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review each suggestion and accept or discard">
    Work through the suggestions one at a time. Click **Accept** to replace your text, **Edit** to refine the suggestion before applying it, or **Discard** to keep your original. You stay in full control of every word that ends up in the final document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the updated resume">
    When you're satisfied, click **Export**. Choose PDF for direct submission or DOCX if you need to do final formatting in a word processor. The workbench saves the optimized version as a new revision so your original is never overwritten.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always read every AI suggestion carefully before accepting it. The optimizer produces a draft, not a finished product. It may misinterpret the scope of a role or suggest language that doesn't accurately reflect your experience. Accepting inaccurate content — even well-written content — can create inconsistencies that surface awkwardly in an interview.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Autumn Job Workbench is local-first. Your resume files, AI scores, and JD match results are stored only on your device — no resume content is sent to an external server outside of the AI inference call you configure.
</Note>

## Maintain Multiple Tailored Versions

As your search expands across different role types or industries, you'll want a separate tailored resume for each track rather than re-optimizing the same document repeatedly. The workbench lets you duplicate any existing resume and rename it (for example, "Product Manager — Enterprise SaaS" or "Senior PM — Fintech"). Each version keeps its own revision history so you can roll back any change.

<Tip>
  Save a "master" resume containing your complete work history and all skills. Use it as the source when duplicating for a new role track, then run the JD match on the copy. This keeps your master intact and gives every tailored version a clean starting point.
</Tip>
