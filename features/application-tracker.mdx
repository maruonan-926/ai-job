Autumn Job Workbench's Application Tracker gives you a bird's-eye view of every role you are pursuing. Each application lives as a card on a Kanban board that you move through stages as your search progresses — so nothing slips through the cracks and you always know exactly where you stand.

## The Kanban Board

Your board is organized into six columns that mirror a real hiring funnel:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Pending" icon="clock">
    Roles you have identified but not yet applied to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Applied" icon="paper-plane">
    Applications you have submitted and are waiting to hear back from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Written Test" icon="file-pen">
    Roles that have sent you an assessment or take-home challenge.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Interview" icon="comments">
    Active interview loops — supports multiple sequential rounds on one card.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Offer" icon="briefcase">
    Roles where you have received a formal offer to review or negotiate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Closed" icon="circle-xmark">
    Applications that ended in a rejection or that you withdrew from.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  All application data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a remote server — your job search stays completely private.
</Note>

## Add a New Application

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the board">
    Navigate to **Application Tracker** in the left sidebar. Click the **+ Add Application** button in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Complete the application form:
    - **Company** — the name of the hiring organization
    - **Role** — the exact job title you are applying for
    - **Application Date** — the date you submitted or plan to submit
    - **Base City** — the primary location of the role (or "Remote")
    - **Channel** — how you found or applied (e.g., LinkedIn, referral, company website)
    - **Notes** — any free-text context, such as a recruiter's name or interview tips
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the card">
    Click **Save**. The new card appears in the **Pending** column by default, ready to be moved when you submit your application.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Move an Application to a New Stage

You can update an application's status in two ways:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Drag and Drop">
    Click and hold any card, then drag it horizontally into the target column. Release to drop — the card snaps into position and the status updates instantly.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Edit the Card">
    Click on a card to open its detail panel. Use the **Status** dropdown to select a new stage, then click **Save Changes**. This is useful when you are on a smaller screen or want to update other fields at the same time.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Star High-Priority Roles

Click the **star icon** (☆) in the top-right corner of any card to mark it as high priority. The star turns gold and the card receives a subtle gold highlight so it stands out on the board. Click the star again to remove the priority mark.

<Tip>
  Use stars to spotlight the two or three roles you are most excited about — then focus your prep energy there first.
</Tip>

## Sort and Filter Your Pipeline

Use the toolbar above the board to narrow your view:

<Accordion title="Filter by Status">
  Click **Filter → Status** and select one or more columns to show only cards in those stages. All other columns are hidden until you clear the filter.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Filter by Company">
  Type a company name into the **Search** bar. Matching cards are highlighted and non-matching cards are dimmed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sort by Date or Priority">
  Open **Sort** and choose **Application Date** (newest or oldest first) or **Priority** (starred cards first). The sort applies within each column independently.
</Accordion>

## Closed and Rejected Applications

When you move a card into the **Closed** column, it is automatically grayed out to visually separate ended applications from active ones. Closed cards are still fully searchable and retain all their notes — they are never deleted unless you explicitly remove them.
