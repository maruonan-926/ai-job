Interview Prep turns the anxiety of interview preparation into a structured practice loop. Generate questions tailored to a specific job description, rehearse with a mock session that gives you immediate feedback, and log every real interview you attend so the AI can help you improve round by round.

## Generate Questions from a Job Description

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Interview Prep">
    Select **Interview Prep** from the left sidebar, then click **+ Generate Questions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste a job description">
    Copy the full job description for the role you are preparing for and paste it into the **Job Description** field. The more complete the JD, the more targeted the questions will be.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and review">
    Click **Generate**. Autumn Job Workbench produces **8–12 targeted questions** drawn from the responsibilities and requirements in the JD. Each question comes with a **reference answer** — a model response you can study, adapt, and make your own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Save the generated question set to a specific application in your tracker so your prep materials stay organized alongside the role. See the [Prepare for an Interview guide](/guides/prepare-interview) for a full workflow.
</Tip>

## Log a Real Interview

After attending an interview, record what happened so the AI can help you debrief and improve.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new interview record">
    Click **+ Log Interview** and fill in the header details:
    - **Company** — the organization you interviewed with
    - **Round** — e.g., Phone Screen, Technical Round 1, Final Panel
    - **Date** — when the interview took place
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions and answers">
    List the questions you were asked and, for each one, write down how you answered. You can also attach questions from a previously generated question set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record feedback and self-rating">
    In the **Feedback** field, note any comments the interviewer made. Then set a **Self-Rating** from 1–5 to capture how confident you felt about your performance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the record">
    Click **Save**. The interview is added to your history and the AI debrief runs automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## AI Debrief

After you save an interview record, the AI immediately generates a structured debrief that covers three areas:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Strengths" icon="thumbs-up">
    What you did well — clear communication, strong examples, good structure in your answers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weaknesses" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Where your responses fell short — vague answers, missing context, or topics you avoided.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Actionable Improvements" icon="arrow-trend-up">
    Specific things to do before your next round — practice a certain topic, add a metric to a story, or refine a particular answer.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The debrief is saved with the interview record so you can revisit it at any time.

## Mock Interview Session

Practice answering questions before a real interview to build confidence and refine your responses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a mock session">
    Open a saved question set and click **Start Mock Session**. The session presents one question at a time in a distraction-free view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to each question">
    Type your answer into the text field and click **Submit Answer**. If your browser supports audio input, click the microphone icon to respond verbally — your speech is transcribed automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive AI feedback">
    After each answer, the AI provides immediate feedback on:
    - **Relevance** — whether your answer addressed what was actually asked
    - **Structure** — how well organized your response was (e.g., use of STAR method)
    - **Depth** — whether you provided enough detail and concrete examples
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue or skip">
    Click **Next Question** to move on, or **Try Again** to re-answer the same question with the feedback in mind.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Audio input availability depends on your browser's microphone permissions. If the microphone icon is grayed out, check that you have granted microphone access in your browser settings and that your device has a working input source.
</Note>

## Review Past Analyses

<Accordion title="Browse your interview history">
  Click **Interview History** to see a chronological list of every logged interview and mock session. Each entry shows the company, round, date, and your self-rating at a glance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Re-read a debrief">
  Click on any logged interview to reopen the full record — questions, your answers, interviewer feedback, and the AI debrief are all preserved exactly as saved.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Track improvement over time">
  The **Progress** tab within Interview Prep charts your self-ratings and AI feedback scores across all logged sessions, so you can see whether your responses are improving round by round.
</Accordion>
