JD Matching takes the guesswork out of tailoring your application. Paste a job description, choose which version of your resume to compare it against, and get a detailed breakdown of where you fit well and where you need to strengthen your case — all in seconds.

## Run a JD Match

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open JD Matching">
    Select **JD Matching** from the left sidebar, then click **+ New Match**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the job description">
    Copy the full job description from the company's careers page and paste it into the **Job Description** text area. Include the responsibilities, requirements, and preferred qualifications sections for the most accurate analysis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a resume version">
    Use the **Resume** dropdown to pick which of your saved resume versions to compare. If you have not uploaded a resume yet, head to [Resume Management](/features/resume-management) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the analysis">
    Click **Analyze Match**. Results appear within a few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Your Results

### Overall Match Percentage

The top of the results panel shows a single overall match score — for example, **74% Match**. This reflects how well your selected resume aligns with the requirements stated in the job description.

### Five-Axis Radar Chart

Below the overall score, a radar chart breaks down your fit across five dimensions so you can see at a glance where your profile is strongest and where gaps exist:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Skills" icon="wrench">
    How well your listed technical and soft skills align with those required or preferred in the JD.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Experience" icon="briefcase">
    Whether your years of experience and the types of roles you have held match the seniority and background the JD calls for.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Education" icon="graduation-cap">
    Alignment between your educational background and any degree or certification requirements in the JD.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keywords" icon="tags">
    Coverage of specific terms, tools, and phrases the employer used — important for ATS screening.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Project Relevance" icon="diagram-project">
    How closely the projects and accomplishments on your resume relate to the work described in the JD.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Matched Skills vs. Missing Skills

Beneath the radar chart, you will find two lists side by side:

- **Matched Skills** — skills and qualifications present on your resume that the JD explicitly mentions. Each matched item is marked with a green checkmark.
- **Missing Skills** — requirements or preferred qualifications in the JD that do not appear on your current resume. Each missing item is marked with a red indicator.

Use the missing skills list as a direct checklist: if you genuinely have a skill that is absent from your resume, add it. If you do not yet have it, consider it a development priority.

## AI Improvement Suggestions

Scroll past the skills lists to find **AI Suggestions** — a set of concrete, prioritized recommendations generated from your gap analysis. Each suggestion explains:

1. What is missing or underdeveloped relative to the JD
2. How to address it (e.g., add a specific skill, rewrite a bullet point with a relevant keyword, or highlight a project more prominently)

<Tip>
  Apply AI suggestions directly by heading to Resume Management and running the optimizer with this JD pasted as your target. See the [Manage Applications guide](/guides/manage-applications) for a step-by-step workflow.
</Tip>

## Saved Match Results

Every match you run is saved automatically. To review a past result:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Match History">
    Click **Match History** in the JD Matching section to see a list of all previous analyses, sorted by date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a saved result">
    Click on any entry to reopen the full results panel, including the radar chart, skills lists, and AI suggestions — exactly as they appeared when the match was first run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-run if needed">
    If you have updated your resume since the original match, click **Re-run Match** to generate a fresh analysis using the same JD and your latest resume version.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The match score is an AI estimate based on text analysis. It is a useful guide for prioritizing your effort, but it is not a guarantee of how a recruiter or ATS will evaluate your application. Always apply your own judgment alongside the score.
</Note>
