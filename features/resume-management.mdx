Autumn Job Workbench's Resume Management gives you a single place to store every version of your resume, understand exactly how strong each one is, and improve it with AI-powered suggestions. Whether you maintain one master resume or a dozen tailored variants, you can keep them organized, scored, and export-ready.

## Upload a Resume

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Resume Management">
    Select **Resume Management** from the left sidebar, then click **+ Upload Resume**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your file">
    Select a file from your device. Autumn Job Workbench accepts:

    - **PDF** (.pdf)
    - **Word document** (.docx, .doc)
    - **Markdown** (.md)
    - **Plain text** (.txt)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and confirm">
    Click **Upload**. The file is parsed and a new resume card appears in your library, ready to be named and scored.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Name and Label Your Versions

Give every resume a clear, descriptive label so you can tell them apart at a glance. Click the pencil icon on any resume card and enter a name such as:

- `Software Engineer — Backend`
- `PM — Product`
- `Data Analyst — Finance Sector`

Good labels make it easy to pick the right version when running a JD match or applying to a specific role.

## AI Resume Scoring

Click **Score Resume** on any resume card to run an AI analysis. The scorer evaluates your resume across five dimensions, each rated out of 10:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Structure Completeness" icon="list-check">
    Checks that all key sections are present — contact info, summary, experience, education, and skills.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Content Quality" icon="star">
    Assesses clarity, relevance, and the strength of the language used throughout.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quantified Achievements" icon="chart-line">
    Looks for measurable impact: numbers, percentages, and concrete outcomes in your bullet points.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keyword Optimization" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Identifies industry and role-relevant keywords present in your resume relative to common expectations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Concise Expression" icon="scissors">
    Flags wordy phrasing, redundant sections, and opportunities to tighten your writing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### View Score Details and Suggestions

After scoring, click **View Details** to expand the full breakdown. Each dimension shows:

1. Its individual score out of 10
2. A short explanation of why that score was given
3. Specific, actionable improvement suggestions

An overall score (the average across all five dimensions) is displayed at the top of the detail panel for a quick read on resume health.

## AI Resume Optimizer

The optimizer goes beyond scoring — it proposes concrete rewrites you can accept or reject one at a time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the optimizer">
    On any scored resume, click **Optimize Resume**. Optionally, paste a target job description to align the suggestions to that specific role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review proposed changes">
    The optimizer presents a list of suggested edits. Each suggestion shows:
    - The **original text** (crossed out in red)
    - The **proposed replacement** (highlighted in green)
    - A brief note explaining the change (e.g., *"STAR-method rewrite — adds quantified outcome"* or *"Keyword alignment — matches JD requirement"*)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or reject each change">
    Click **Accept** to apply a suggestion or **Reject** to keep your original text. Work through the full list at your own pace — you are always in control of what goes into your resume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the updated version">
    Once you have reviewed all suggestions, click **Save Changes**. The optimizer creates an updated version of your resume; your original is preserved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before running the optimizer, complete a JD match first — this gives the AI a precise target to optimize toward. Learn more in the [Optimize Your Resume guide](/guides/optimize-resume).
</Tip>

## Export and Download Your Resume

To download a finished resume, open its card and click **Export**. Choose your preferred format:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="PDF">
    Generates a clean, print-ready PDF. Recommended for most job applications.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Word (.docx)">
    Exports an editable Word document if the employer requires a Word file or you want to make final tweaks in a word processor.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Markdown">
    Exports the raw Markdown source — useful for pasting into developer portfolios or version-controlling your resume in a Git repository.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Exporting as PDF locks the formatting to your current settings. If you plan to run the optimizer again, finish all edits before exporting the final copy.
</Warning>
