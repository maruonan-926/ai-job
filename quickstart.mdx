Autumn Job Workbench is designed to get out of your way and let you start tracking immediately. This guide walks you through the five essential steps to go from a fresh install to a fully running job search workflow — no account, no configuration required to get started, and no data sent anywhere.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari — all current versions supported)
- At least one resume file ready to upload (PDF, Word `.docx`, or Markdown) **or** your resume text available to paste
- *(Optional)* An API key from an OpenAI-compatible AI provider such as [DeepSeek](https://platform.deepseek.com) or [Zhipu GLM](https://open.bigmodel.cn) if you want to use AI-powered features

<Note>
  AI features such as JD matching and interview question generation require an API key, but the Application Tracker and Resume Management modules work fully offline without one.
</Note>

## Setup Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Workbench in Your Browser">
    Navigate to the Autumn Job Workbench URL provided with your installation (for example, `http://localhost:5173` if you're running it locally). The workbench loads entirely in your browser — no login screen, no onboarding survey.

    You'll land on the **Dashboard**, which gives you a summary view of your applications, recent activity, and quick-action buttons. Take a moment to orient yourself with the sidebar navigation on the left, which links to each of the core modules.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Your First Resume">
    Navigate to **Resume Management** in the sidebar.

    Click **Add Resume Version** and choose how you'd like to provide your resume:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Upload a File">
        Click **Upload File** and select a PDF, Word (`.docx`), or Markdown (`.md`) file from your device. AJW parses the file and stores it as a named resume version. Give it a clear label — for example, `Software Engineer – General v1` — so you can identify it later.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Paste Text">
        Click **Paste Text**, then copy and paste the full content of your resume into the text editor. Apply a version label and click **Save**. This is useful if your resume lives in a Google Doc or another format that doesn't export cleanly.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Once saved, your resume version appears in the Resume Management list. You can add as many versions as you like — AJW stores them all locally and never overwrites an existing version unless you explicitly do so.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your First Application Record">
    Navigate to **Application Tracker** in the sidebar.

    Click **New Application**. Fill in the following fields:

    | Field | What to Enter |
    |---|---|
    | **Company** | The name of the company you're applying to |
    | **Role** | The job title as it appears in the posting |
    | **Status** | Leave as **Pending** for a role you haven't applied to yet, or set to **Applied** if you've already submitted |
    | **Resume Version** | Select the resume version you just added |
    | **Notes** | Any early context — referrals, deadlines, interesting details |

    Click **Save**. Your first application record now appears in the tracker table. You'll move it through the status pipeline — Pending → Applied → Written Test → Interview → Offer → Closed — as your process progresses.

    <Tip>
      You can paste the job description directly into the **Job Description** field on the application record. Storing it here makes it easy to run a JD match later without hunting for the original posting.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Your AI Model">
    Navigate to **Settings** → **AI Settings** (or go directly to [AI Settings](/configuration/ai-settings)).

    AJW works with any OpenAI-compatible API endpoint. To connect your provider:

    1. Enter your **API Base URL** — for example, `https://api.deepseek.com/v1` for DeepSeek
    2. Paste your **API Key** in the key field
    3. Enter the **Model Name** you want to use — for example, `deepseek-chat` or `glm-4`
    4. Click **Test Connection** to verify the key is valid and the model responds
    5. Click **Save**

    <Tip>
      DeepSeek and Zhipu GLM offer low-cost API tiers that are well-suited for resume analysis and question generation. See [AI Settings](/configuration/ai-settings) for a full list of tested providers and recommended model names.
    </Tip>

    <Warning>
      Your API key is stored in your browser's local storage and never sent to Autumn Job Workbench servers. However, treat your API key like a password — do not share your browser profile or export your local storage to untrusted parties.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run Your First JD Match">
    Navigate to **JD Matching** in the sidebar, or open the application record you created in Step 3 and click **Run JD Match** from the record detail view.

    1. Select the **Resume Version** you want to evaluate
    2. Confirm the **Job Description** is populated (paste it now if you didn't add it to the application record earlier)
    3. Click **Analyze Match**

    AJW sends your resume and the job description to your configured AI model and returns a structured match report. The report highlights:

    - **Match score** — an overall percentage alignment
    - **Keyword gaps** — skills and terms in the JD that don't appear in your resume
    - **Strength areas** — parts of your background that align strongly with the role
    - **Suggested edits** — specific, actionable resume tweaks to improve your score

    Review the suggestions and use them to refine your resume version before submitting your application.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're Up and Running

You've completed the core setup loop: resume added, application recorded, AI connected, and your first match analyzed. From here, explore the rest of the workbench at your own pace.
