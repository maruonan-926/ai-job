Autumn Job Workbench (AJW) is a local-first job search management tool designed to bring every part of your recruiting season into one place. This page introduces what AJW does, who it's built for, and the core features you'll use throughout your job search — from the moment you tailor your first resume to the day you evaluate a final offer.

## What Is Autumn Job Workbench?

Autumn Job Workbench gives you a structured, distraction-free workspace to run your entire job search. Instead of juggling spreadsheets, cloud documents, and scattered browser tabs, you manage everything — applications, resumes, AI-powered job description analysis, and interview prep — inside a single interface that runs entirely in your browser with all data stored on your device.

<Note>
  **Your data never leaves your device.** Autumn Job Workbench stores all application records, resume versions, match results, and notes in your browser's local storage. No account sign-up is required, and nothing is transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly configure an AI provider API key.
</Note>

## Who Is It For?

AJW is built for anyone actively navigating a job search who wants more structure and insight than a plain spreadsheet can offer:

- **New graduates** entering the job market for the first time and managing a high volume of applications
- **Experienced professionals** running a focused, targeted search across a handful of high-priority roles
- **Career changers** who need to tailor multiple resume versions to very different job descriptions
- **Anyone who values privacy** and prefers their job search data to stay on their own machine

## Key Value Props

- **All-in-one workspace** — track applications, store resumes, analyze job descriptions, and prepare for interviews without switching tools
- **Local-first privacy** — your data lives on your device; no account, no cloud sync, no data collection
- **AI-assisted, not AI-dependent** — powerful AI features are available when you connect an API key, but every core feature works without one
- **Version-aware resume management** — store and compare multiple resume versions so you always know which copy you sent where
- **Structured status pipeline** — move applications through a clear set of stages so nothing slips through the cracks
