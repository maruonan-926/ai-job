Autumn Job Workbench is ready to use the moment you open it — no sign-up, no subscription, and no cloud account required. This guide walks you through the first-launch experience so you can personalize the workbench and start managing your job search right away.

<Note>
  Autumn Job Workbench runs entirely in your browser as a local application. You do not need to create an account or provide an email address to use any feature.
</Note>

## Open the Workbench

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch the app">
    Navigate to the Autumn Job Workbench URL in your browser (for example, `http://localhost:3000` if you are running it locally, or the hosted address your administrator provided). The app loads directly — there is no login screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dismiss the welcome overlay">
    On your first visit, a welcome overlay introduces the main sections of the workbench. Read through it and click **Get Started** to proceed to the main dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Your Display Name and Profile

Your display name appears in the top navigation bar and inside generated documents such as cover letters. Setting it now ensures every document the workbench produces uses the correct name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile Settings">
    Click your avatar icon in the top-right corner of the dashboard, then select **Profile & Preferences** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your display name">
    Type your full name (or the name you use professionally) into the **Display Name** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add optional details">
    Optionally fill in your **Location** and **Primary Contact Email**. The workbench uses these to pre-populate fields when you draft application materials, saving you from retyping them each time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your profile">
    Click **Save Profile**. Your details are stored locally on this device and are never uploaded to any external server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose Your Target Job Tracks

Selecting one or more job tracks tells the workbench which scoring rubrics, keyword libraries, and interview question banks to prioritize. You can update this selection at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Job Tracks panel">
    In **Profile & Preferences**, scroll down to the **Job Tracks** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your tracks">
    Check the boxes next to the tracks that match your goals. Available tracks include:

    - **Software Engineering** — backend, frontend, full-stack, mobile, and infrastructure roles
    - **Product Management** — PM, APM, and technical PM positions
    - **Operations & Strategy** — business operations, strategy, and chief-of-staff roles
    - **Finance & Accounting** — investment banking, FP&A, accounting, and corporate finance
    - **Data & Analytics** — data science, data engineering, and BI roles
    - **Design** — UX/UI, product design, and visual design positions
    - **Marketing & Growth** — content, performance marketing, and growth roles
    - **Other / General** — a flexible track for roles that span multiple categories

    You may select more than one track if you are exploring several directions simultaneously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your selection">
    Click **Save Tracks**. The dashboard now surfaces relevant templates, keyword suggestions, and interview prompts for your chosen tracks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you are undecided, start with **Other / General**. You can always return to this panel and add specific tracks once your search becomes more focused.
</Tip>

## Adjust Display Preferences

### Light and Dark Mode

The workbench follows your operating system's appearance setting by default. To override it manually:

1. Go to **Profile & Preferences → Display**.
2. Under **Color Scheme**, choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System Default**.
3. The change takes effect immediately — no page reload is needed.

### Language

The interface language defaults to the locale detected from your browser. To switch it:

1. In **Profile & Preferences → Display**, open the **Language** dropdown.
2. Select your preferred language from the list.
3. Click **Apply**. The page reloads with the new language applied.

<Note>
  AI-generated content (resume feedback, JD analysis, interview answers) is returned in the language of the text you submit, not the interface language. If you submit an English resume, you will receive English feedback regardless of your interface language setting.
</Note>
