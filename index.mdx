## How It Works

Autumn Job Workbench guides you through every stage of a successful job search in four steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Your Resumes">
    Upload or paste one or more resume versions. The workbench stores them so you can compare, score, and optimize each one independently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Your Applications">
    Create an application record for each role you apply to. Move cards through the pipeline — Applied, Written Test, Interview, Offer — as you progress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match & Optimize">
    Paste a job description to see how well your resume fits. The AI highlights matched skills, flags gaps, and suggests targeted improvements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare & Evaluate">
    Practice interview questions predicted from the JD, log feedback after each round, and score competing offers on salary, growth, and culture.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Your data stays on your device. Autumn Job Workbench stores all resumes, applications, and interview notes locally — nothing is uploaded to a cloud server without your permission.
</Tip>
